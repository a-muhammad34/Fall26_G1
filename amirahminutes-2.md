### Different Users

This app is mainly for new ASU students who are trying to find their way as the campus is a new environment for them. However, ASU Navigate can be used by anyone who needs some direction on campus. This includes students, professors, parents, friends of students, and other visitors. They can use this app to find a class, meet up with another student, find an event, or even just to explore the campus.
